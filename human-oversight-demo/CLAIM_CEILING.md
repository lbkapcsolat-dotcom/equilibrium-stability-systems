# Claim Ceiling

This public demo may claim only:

- bounded executable synthetic human-oversight harness
- frozen capability model and role/authority semantics
- synthetic adversarial scenario mechanism
- deterministic replay concept and evidence-bound decision framing

It must not claim:

- general human competence
- organizational resilience
- AI Act compliance
- production readiness
- runtime admission
- certification
- empirical human validation before the real-participant pilot is completed
