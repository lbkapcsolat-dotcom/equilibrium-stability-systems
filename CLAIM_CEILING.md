# Claim Ceiling

The strongest system-level public statement supported by this repository is:

> Equilibrium Stability Systems is a research-stage architecture composed of bounded, separately testable public and private components concerned with evidence handling, explicit admission logic, reproducibility, and fail-closed decision-support safeguards.

This repository does **not** support claims that ESS is:

- production-ready;
- universally validated;
- a safety certification system;
- a medical, legal, financial, or regulatory decision authority;
- a general truth detector;
- proven reliable outside the explicit boundaries of its component evidence.

## Composition rule

```text
VERIFIED(component_i)
does not imply
VERIFIED(ESS)
```

System-level claims require system-level evidence.

## Preferred language

Use:

- "research-stage"
- "bounded verification"
- "tested for the stated scope"
- "experimental"
- "public research artifact"

Avoid:

- "fully validated"
- "production safe"
- "guaranteed"
- "universally reliable"
- "proven system-wide"

unless future evidence explicitly supports such a statement.
